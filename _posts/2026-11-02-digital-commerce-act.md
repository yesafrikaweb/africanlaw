<div id="section-1" class="section-block">
    <span class="section-number">1</span>
    <h2 class="section-header">Short title and commencement</h2>
    <p class="provision-text">(1) This Act may be cited as the Digital Commerce and Data Protection Act.</p>
    <p class="provision-text">(2) This Act shall come into force on such date as the Minister may by notice appoint.</p>
</div>

<div id="section-2" class="section-block">
    <span class="section-number">2</span>
    <h2 class="section-header">Interpretation and definitions</h2>
    <p class="provision-text">(1) In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—</p>
    <p class="provision-text"><strong>"data controller"</strong> means a person who determines the purposes and manner of data processing.</p>
</div>
